Vinegar coleslaw

Finely shredded green cabbage and carrot, salted briefly to pull out surface water, then dressed with cider vinegar, a little sugar, oil, dijon, and celery seed. Crunchy, sharp, and mayonnaise-free, so it cuts rich seafood and fried food instead of adding to it.

Before you start

Read this first — the long-lead and coordination steps that need to be moving before you start prepping.

The salted cabbage needs 20 minutes to release water, and the dressed slaw is best after another 20 to 30 minutes. Start it before anything else on the plate; it holds well for up to 3 days.

Step 1 · Vegetables

  1. 450 g green cabbage, cored and finely shredded, shredded 2 to 3 mm thick with a knife or mandoline — (about ½ medium head)
  2. 80 g carrot, coarsely grated — (1 medium)
  3. 6 g kosher salt

Step 2 · Dressing

  1. 45 g apple cider vinegar
  2. 12 g granulated sugar
  3. 5 g dijon mustard
  4. 1 g celery seed
  5. 1 g black pepper, freshly ground
  6. 30 g neutral oil
  7. 15 g scallion, thinly sliced — (2 scallions)

Method

  1. 1
    Salt the vegetables. Toss the 450 g shredded cabbage and 80 g grated carrot with the 6 g / 1½ tsp kosher salt in a colander set over a bowl. Let stand 20 minutes. The salt draws water out of the cut surfaces by osmosis; that water would otherwise dilute the dressing and pool at the bottom of the bowl. The cabbage softens slightly at the edges but keeps its crunch.
  2. 2
    Make the dressing. In a large bowl, whisk the 45 g / 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar, 12 g / 1 tbsp granulated sugar, 5 g / 1 tsp dijon mustard, 1 g / ½ tsp celery seed, and 1 g / ½ tsp black pepper until the sugar dissolves. Whisk in the 30 g / 2 tbsp neutral oil. Technique: Vinaigrette emulsion
  3. 3
    Squeeze and dress. Grab the cabbage by handfuls and squeeze firmly to press out the released liquid, then drop it into the dressing. Add the 15 g / 2 scallions and toss well. Taste a strand: it should be clearly sour, lightly sweet, and seasoned without needing more salt (the brief salting leaves about 1% behind). If it reads too sharp, add sugar by the ½ teaspoon.
  4. 4
    Rest. Refrigerate 20 to 30 minutes, tossing once, so the dressing seasons the cabbage through. Toss again just before serving.

Modifications

Pivots that change the dish's character — protein swaps, regional variants, dietary recastings.

  • Texture dressing→creamy coleslaw

    Cut the oil to 15 g / 1 tbsp and whisk 60 g / ¼ cup mayonnaise into the dressing. Softer and richer; better beside fried chicken or pulled pork than beside crab cakes.

  • Heat heat→Chesapeake slaw

    Add 1 g / ¼ tsp Old Bay-style seasoning and a pinch of red pepper flakes to the dressing.

Substitutions

1:1 ingredient swaps where the dish stays the same dish.

  • green cabbage → savoy or napa cabbage Both are more tender and release water faster; salt them for 10 minutes instead of 20, and serve within a day.
  • apple cider vinegar → white wine vinegar Same amount. Cleaner and less fruity.

Make it from scratch

Store-bought is fine, but each of these has a homemade version that earns the time.

  • → dijon mustard make it yourself Fresh mustard gives the dressing a sharper edge and stabilizes the oil a little longer.

Notes

The pre-salt is the step most slaw recipes skip, and it is why theirs are watery after an hour. Salting first means the slaw is still crunchy on day 3.

This is a side, so there is no glutamate source on purpose: its job on the plate is acid and crunch against something rich and savory.
