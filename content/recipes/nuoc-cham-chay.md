notes: |
  Why soy plus shiitake. Fish sauce reads deeply savory because it carries both free glutamate and inosinate-type nucleotides, which amplify each other. Soy alone gives glutamate but tastes flat and grainy in a thin dip. Adding shiitake soaking liquid supplies guanylate, and the combined effect is much stronger than either ingredient alone. This is the same synergy behind kombu and katsuobushi in dashi.

  Coconut water. In the south, coconut water replaces plain water in many dipping sauces and braises. Its natural sugars and potassium soften the soy's edge and make the sauce taste rounder.

  Storage. Keeps 4 days refrigerated. Coconut water ferments faster than plain water; a sour, fizzy smell means discard.
