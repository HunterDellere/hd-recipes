steps:
  - text: 'Heat the oven to 220 °C / 425 °F with two rimmed sheet pans inside, on racks in the upper and lower thirds.'
    time_min: 15
  - text: 'Cut the squash. Trim both ends of the 1600 g / 4 delicata squash, halve them lengthwise, and scrape out the seeds with a metal spoon. Cut each half crosswise into 1.5 cm / ⅝ inch half-moons. Do not peel: delicata skin is thin enough to turn tender in the oven, and it holds each slice together.'
    time_min: 10
  - text: 'Season. Toss the slices in a large bowl with the 45 g / 3 tbsp olive oil, 8 g / 2 tsp kosher salt and 1 g / ½ tsp black pepper. Salting before roasting draws a little surface moisture that evaporates in the first minutes and seasons the flesh through.'
    time_min: 2
  - text: 'Roast. Take the hot pans out and spread the squash in one layer, cut faces down and not touching. Crowded slices steam instead of browning, so use both pans. Roast 15 minutes, until the undersides are deep golden. Flip each slice with a thin metal spatula, swap the pans between racks, and roast 8 to 12 minutes more, until a paring knife slides through the skin without resistance.'
    technique: 'high-heat-dry-roasting'
    time_min: 27
  - text: 'Make the hot honey while the squash roasts. Warm the 85 g / ¼ cup honey and the 3 g / 1 tsp red pepper flakes in a small saucepan over low heat until the honey is fluid and just starts to bubble at the edge, about 2 minutes. Take it off the heat, stir in the 15 g / 1 tbsp cider vinegar, and let it steep 10 minutes. Capsaicin dissolves in the warm honey, and the vinegar thins it so it drizzles and keeps the sweetness from dominating.'
    time_min: 12
  - text: 'Toast the pepitas. In a dry skillet over medium heat, toast the 50 g / ⅓ cup pepitas with the 5 g / 1 tsp olive oil and the 1 g / ¼ tsp kosher salt, shaking the pan, until they puff, pop and turn golden at the edges, 3 to 4 minutes. Tip them onto a plate to stop the cooking.'
    technique: 'dry-toasting'
    time_min: 4
  - text: 'Finish. Arrange the squash on a platter. Zest the 1 lime over it, then squeeze over about 30 g / 2 tbsp of the juice. Drizzle with the hot honey, then scatter the pepitas, the 60 g / ½ cup cotija, the 8 g / ⅓ cup cilantro and the 1 g / ¼ tsp flaky salt. Taste a slice: it should be sweet first, then hot, sour and salty. If it tastes mostly sweet, add more lime.'
    time_min: 3
