steps:
  - text: 'Start the fennel. Heat the oven to 220 C / 425 F. On a rimmed sheet pan toss the 600 g / 2 bulbs fennel wedges and 150 g / 1 small red onion wedges with the 40 g / 3 tbsp olive oil for the fennel and the 6 g / 2 tsp kosher salt for the fennel. Spread cut-side down and roast 20 minutes until the undersides are browned and the wedges are starting to soften.'
    technique: 'high-heat-dry-roasting'
    time_min: 20
  - text: 'Season the fish while the fennel roasts. Season the 1100 g / 2 scored orata inside, outside, and into the slashes with the 11 g / 1 tbsp kosher salt for the fish and the zest of the 250 g orange. Fill each cavity with half the 4 g / 1½ tsp crushed fennel seed and half of the orange rounds. Rub the outside with the 14 g / 1 tbsp olive oil for the fish. Let sit at room temperature until the fennel is ready.'
    technique: 'fish-skin-scoring'
    time_min: 8
  - text: 'Make the anchovy oil. In a small saucepan over low heat, warm the 40 g / 3 tbsp olive oil for the anchovy oil with the 12 g / 4 chopped anchovy fillets, the 12 g / 3 sliced garlic cloves, and the 1 g / ½ tsp pepper flakes. Mash until the anchovies dissolve and the garlic is soft and pale gold, 3 to 4 minutes. Off heat, stir in the 10 g / 2 tsp white wine vinegar.'
    technique: 'blooming-aromatics'
    time_min: 4
  - text: 'Roast the fish. Flip the fennel, push it to the edges, and lay the fish in the center. Tuck the orange wedges and the 80 g / ½ cup olives around them. Spoon half the anchovy oil over the fennel (not the fish skin, which should stay dry to crisp). Roast 16 to 20 minutes until the thickest part of the fish reads 55 C / 131 F. Orata is deeper-bodied than branzino and takes a few minutes longer.'
    time_min: 18
  - text: 'Finish. Squeeze 2 of the roasted orange wedges over the fennel; the caramelized juice mixes with the anchovy oil and fish drippings into a pan sauce. Spoon the remaining anchovy oil over the fish, scatter the chopped fennel fronds, and serve from the pan. Taste the fennel with a piece of fish and olive: the olive and anchovy should read as savory salt, the orange as bright sweetness. Add a few drops more vinegar if it reads sweet.'
    time_min: 3

notes: |
  Staggered roast. Fennel is fibrous and needs time for its pectin to soften and its sugars to caramelize; a whole bream needs only 18 minutes. Roasting them together from the start gives you either raw fennel or overcooked fish. The 20-minute head start lines them up.

  Fennel twice. The bulb gives sweetness and anise after caramelization; the seed in the cavity gives a sharper, more concentrated anethole note that perfumes the flesh. Fronds at the end bring it back fresh.

  Keeping the skin dry. Anchovy oil spooned over the skin before roasting keeps it soft. Oil the skin lightly, sauce the fennel, and save the rest of the anchovy oil for after the fish comes out.
