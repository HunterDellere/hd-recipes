steps:
  - text: 'Macerate the shallot. Combine the 15 g / 1 tbsp minced shallot and the 10 g / 2 tsp lemon juice in a small bowl and let sit 5 minutes. The acid softens the raw sulfur bite of the shallot so it reads sweet and sharp instead of hot.'
    time_min: 5
  - text: 'Mix. Add the 130 g / ½ cup + 1 tbsp mayonnaise, 30 g / 8 minced cornichons, 5 g / 1 tsp cornichon brine, 10 g / 1 tbsp chopped capers, 3 g / 1 tbsp dill, 1 g / ½ tsp lemon zest, 4 g / ¾ tsp dijon mustard, and 0.5 g / ¼ tsp black pepper. Stir until evenly flecked.'
    time_min: 3
  - text: 'Rest and adjust. Cover and refrigerate 30 minutes. Taste on a piece of whatever it is going on, not off a spoon: against crab or fried fish it should read clearly sour and briny. If it tastes flat, add brine 1 teaspoon at a time rather than salt; the brine carries salt and acid together.'
    time_min: 30

notes: |
  The sauce is about acid and brine more than mayonnaise. Keep the add-ins high (about 35% of the weight) so each bite has a pickle or caper in it.

  Keeps 5 days refrigerated. The shallot gets stronger after day 3.
