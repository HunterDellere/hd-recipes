steps:
  - text: 'Salt the zucchini. Toss the 500 g / 2 zucchini rounds with the 5 g / 1½ tsp kosher salt for the zucchini in a colander and let drain 20 minutes. Salt pulls water out by osmosis; without this step the zucchini floods the pan and the briam stews instead of roasting. Heat the oven to 190 C / 375 F.'
    time_min: 20
  - text: 'Build the bath. Stir together the 600 g grated tomatoes, 15 g / 1 tbsp tomato paste, 110 g / ½ cup olive oil, 20 g / 5 sliced garlic cloves, 3 g / 1 tbsp rigani, 1 g / ½ tsp pepper, and half the 20 g parsley.'
    time_min: 3
  - text: 'Toss and layer. Pat the zucchini dry. In a large metal roasting pan (about 33 x 23 cm / 13 x 9 in), toss the 500 g potato slices, 200 g / 1 onion, 250 g / 2 peppers, and zucchini with the 8 g / 2½ tsp kosher salt for the vegetables and the tomato bath. Settle the potatoes toward the bottom where the liquid is deepest, and the zucchini on top, where it will brown.'
    time_min: 5
  - text: 'Roast covered. Cover tightly with foil and roast 40 minutes. The trapped steam cooks the potatoes through in the tomato liquid before anything can brown or dry out.'
    time_min: 40
  - text: 'Roast uncovered. Remove the foil, gently turn the vegetables once with a spatula, and roast another 35 to 40 minutes until the liquid has reduced to a thick tomato-oil sauce at the bottom of the pan and the top edges of the 500 g zucchini and 250 g peppers are browned. A knife should slide through a potato slice with no resistance.'
    technique: 'high-heat-dry-roasting'
    time_min: 38
  - text: 'Finish and rest. Sprinkle the 10 g / 2 tsp red wine vinegar over the pan and scatter the remaining parsley. Rest at least 15 minutes; briam is better warm than hot, and best at room temperature. Taste and add the 1 g pinch of flaky salt if the vegetables need it.'
    time_min: 15

notes: |
  Why salt the zucchini. Zucchini is about 95% water. Without pre-salting, it releases that water in the oven, diluting the tomato bath so it takes twice as long to reduce and leaves the potatoes boiled rather than roasted. Twenty minutes in salt removes enough to make the difference.

  Cut by density. Potatoes cut thin (6 mm), onions and peppers medium, zucchini thick (1.5 cm). A dense potato and a watery zucchini cut the same thickness finish 30 minutes apart.

  Lathera. The half cup of olive oil is the point of the dish. It emulsifies with the reduced tomato into the sauce, carries the oregano and garlic, and is why briam tastes better at room temperature: the oil-tomato sauce sets and the flavors settle.

  Umami and acid. Grated ripe tomato and a spoon of paste carry the glutamate; the vinegar at the end lifts what slow roasting has rounded off.
